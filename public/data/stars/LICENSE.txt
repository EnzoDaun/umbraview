stars.bin is a subset of the HYG star database, version 4.4, by David Nash
(https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg): the stars down to apparent magnitude 6.5, with their
J2000 direction, magnitude and B-V color index, written by
scripts/prepare-data/prepare_stars.py.
