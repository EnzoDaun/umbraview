stars.bin

Derived from the HYG star catalog, version 4.4, by David Nash (astronexus),
https://codeberg.org/astronexus/hyg (file hyg/CURRENT/hyg_v44.csv.gz).

Changes: kept only stars of visual magnitude 6.5 or brighter, without the Sun; each star was
reduced to its J2000 equatorial unit direction (from rarad and decrad), its magnitude (mag) and
its B-V color index (ci, NaN when the catalog has none), stored as little-endian Float32,
5 numbers per star. Generated by scripts/prepare-data/prepare_stars.py in the UmbraView
repository (https://github.com/EnzoDaun/umbraview).
