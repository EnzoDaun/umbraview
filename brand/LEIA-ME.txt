UMBRA TEAM / UMBRAVIEW - identity kit

Colors
  Black (background)  #050608
  White (light, text) #f4f5f7
  Navy blue (umbra)   #10266a
  Gray (TEAM, lines)  #a0a6b4

Typography
  Fira Sans. UMBRΛ in Medium (500), TEAM in Regular (400), VIEW in Light (300).
  In the SVG and PNG files the text is already converted to curves; there is no need to install the font.
  The "Λ" is the Greek capital letter lambda (U+039B).

Files
  svg/  vectors, transparent background; any PNG comes from here, when the submission asks for one
  favicon/  favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), standalone PNGs, favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon-180.png

  umbra-simbolo            symbol alone (with elevation scale)
  umbra-team-vertical      main lockup, symbol on top
  umbra-team-horizontal    lockup with symbol on the left
  umbraview-horizontal     product lockup
  umbraview-cabecalho      compact version for the site header (simplified symbol)
  ...-mono-branco          one color, for video and dark backgrounds
  ...-mono-preto           one color, for light backgrounds

Usage
  The site uses two of these versions, copied to public/brand/: umbraview-cabecalho (in the header)
  and umbra-simbolo-mono-branco (in the footer). Only what is in public/ is served.
  The color and mono-branco versions were made for a dark background (ideal: #050608).
  Below ~64 px of symbol height, use the favicon or umbraview-cabecalho: the scale and the thin horizon disappear.
  At 16 px the favicon has no horizon line, on purpose.
